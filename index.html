About the Hub

Curating the conversations that move industries forward.

Explore Our Editions

The Sustainable Design Impact Hub is a curated convening series bringing together the people shaping the future of materials, design, and industry.

Across cities and disciplines, we connect founders, scientists, designers, investors, and established industry to exchange knowledge, challenge assumptions, and explore how innovation moves from idea to application.

From Biella to New York and next Paris, each edition responds to the distinct creative and industrial culture of its host city.

Fashion x Deep Tech

Innovation Gallery

A curated presentation of design-forward deep tech built in New York.

The New York gallery spotlighted emerging companies, materials, products, and technologies shaping the future of fashion, manufacturing, mobility, and the built environment. Designed as an elevated exhibition rather than a traditional trade show, the space brought founders and their work into direct conversation with investors, brands, designers, researchers, and industry leaders.

The exhibition emphasized design thinking, technical ambition, and the potential for meaningful environmental and social impact.
